# Corrective Action Requests and Recommendations

During the audit process, the auditor issued 4 corrective action requests, 5 recommendations and 1 carry forward recommendation. The auditor verified that Alcom had addressed all corrective actions requests and as such is satisfied that the correct calculations had been provided.

# Corrective Action Request 1

The auditor noted that some dates had been incorrectly listed due to incorrect date formats (e.g., US date format when it should have been UK date format and vice versa). Subsequently, Alcom reviewed and amended the date formats to be in UK date format.

# Corrective Action Request 2

The auditor noted that some delivery receipts for the biochar sold had not been dated and/or signed by the Purchaser. Subsequently, Alcom requested the relevant Purchasers to date and sign the delivery receipts.

# Corrective Action Request 3

The auditor noted the following issues regarding the LCA inputs and calculations:

- The GHG emissions for machinery had been calculated but not included in the tCOe/tonne of rice husk emissions factor. However, the emissions were considered in the final CORC calculations;
- The quantity of biochar sold had been used, instead of the quantity of biochar produced, to calculate the GHG emissions and the tCOe/tonne of biochar related to production. These calculations impacted the Ebiomass, Eproduction and Estored. Although the impact was insignificant due to the similar values of production and sales in this reporting period;
- The incorrect number of days of operation had been used;
- The quantity of biochar sold was ~1 tonne more than the quantity of biochar produced, as such the quantity of biochar produced had been used as the quantity of biochar sold because it was the most conservative of the two values;
- Some receipts and/or logbooks for rice husk delivery, biochar produced, and biochar sold had been incorrectly or not transcribed to the biochar inventory summary;
- The emissions associated with biochar sales packaging/bags had not been accounted for in the LCA;
- For stages where the quantity of rice husk was not relevant (e.g., during biochar application and bagging), the associated emissions factors in tonne of biochar had been converted to tonne of rice husk, which is then re-converted to tonne of biochar using the ratio of rice husk vs biochar, instead of calculating directly from the tCOe/tonne of biochar.

Subsequently, Alcom reviewed their LCA calculation and amended the above actions.

# Corrective Action Request 4

The auditor noted that Alcom had a procedure to include a “no-double counting clause” in their delivery receipts/invoices for biochar sold, however this procedure had not been implemented. Subsequently, Alcom revised their delivery receipt/invoice template to include the no-double counting clause.

# Recommendation 1

The auditor recommends that Alcom transcribe the gross, tare and net weight in a document (e.g., MS Excel) from the receipts of the delivery of rice husks and biochar to calculate the average weight of loaded and unloaded trucks.

# Recommendations

# Recommendation 2

The auditor recommends that Alcom use a consistent date format (i.e., either UK format or US format) across all documents.

# Recommendation 3

The auditor recommends that Alcom augment their quality assurance and record keeping procedures to ensure that:

- The LCA includes all relevant emissions e.g., biochar packaging bags;
- Calculations in the LCA are peer reviewed; and
- Inputs to the LCA are correct, accurate, well documented and consistent with supporting evidence.

# Recommendation 4

The auditor recommends that Alcom obtain all relevant evidentiary documents to support any claim or assumptions in relation to the LCA calculations (e.g., diesel use factor for biochar application).

# Recommendation 5

The auditor recommends that Alcom enhance their internal sales procedure to ensure that a “no-double counting clause” is included where relevant (e.g., marketing materials, invoices, receipts, etc.) and that the biochar Purchaser is aware of it.

# Carry Forward Recommendation 1

The auditor recommended that Alcom measure the actual electricity consumption of the facility.

Positive Conclusion (Production Output Audit).

# Production Output Audit

The lead auditor is able to express a reasonable assurance opinion that, in all material respects, the quantification of 442 CO2 Removal Certificates (CORCs) for the reporting period 3rd June 2022 to 11th December 2023 are eligible for creation. However, there was an overlap in the period covering the proponent’s previous audit (10 June 2022 to 26 January 2023) and this audit. Therefore, 22 CORCs that were in the previous audit’s reporting period were subtracted from this audit (442 - 22 = 420 CORCs). The audit procedures indicated that an overcalculation of 9 CORCs was made by the audited body.
