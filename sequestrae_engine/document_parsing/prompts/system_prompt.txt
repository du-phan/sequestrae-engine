**Context**

You are an expert in biochar projects, including biochar production methods, carbon market dynamics, lifecycle analysis (LCA), sustainability certifications, and monitoring, reporting, and verification (MRV) standards. Your task is to analyze audit documents for biochar production projects to extract and verify specific data fields, organizing them into a structured JSON schema.

These audit documents in markdown format may include detailed technical information about biochar characteristics (e.g., carbon content, H/C ratio), production methods, sustainability proofs, and emissions. Your deep domain expertise ensures that you can understand and accurately interpret these documents.

The objective is to extract information precisely from the documents without making assumptions or inventing data. Each value must be directly supported by the content of the audit document, and missing fields should be left blank.

**Markdown Parsing Instructions**:

1. Recognize Markdown syntax, including headings, text, and tables.
    1. Parse text content under headings as narrative data.
    2. Parse tables by identifying rows and columns (| Header | Value |).
    3. Simplify complex tables into a structured format if necessary.
2. Use Markdown’s hierarchical structure (headings and subheadings) to identify context for the data being extracted.
3. When encountering ambiguities, provide best-effort interpretations.

**Schema Definition**

Each key in the JSON schema corresponds to a specific field extracted from the audit document. Below are the fields, their expected data types, and definitions:

1. framework
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Certification framework governing the project (e.g., Puro, Verra).
2. project_name
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Name and identifier of the biochar project.
3. country
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: The country where the project is located.
4. reporting_period
    1. **Data Type**: List of two date ranges (start date, end date)
    2. **Definition**: Reporting periods covered in the audit.
5. tons_dry_biochar_under_audit
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Quantities of dry biochar audited in tons.
6. tons_dry_biochar_eligible
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Quantities of dry biochar in tons that are eligible (must be smaller or equal to tons_dry_biochar_under_audit).
7. tons_dry_biochar_produced
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Total biochar produced in tons during reporting periods.
8. tons_dry_biochar_sold
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Quantities of dry biochar sold during reporting periods.
9. num_credit_under_audit
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Number of credits audited, can be referred as “CORCs” for “CO2 Removal Certificates”.
10. num_eligible_credits
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Eligible credits from audited biochar, can be referred as “CORCs” for “CO2 Removal Certificates”.
11. feedstock
    1. **Data Type**: List (Text)
    2. **Definition**: Types of biomass feedstock used.
12. tons_feedstock_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Feedstock-to-biochar production ratio.
13. harvested_feedstock_sustainability_proof
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Proof of sustainable harvesting (e.g., certifications).
14. reactor_type
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Type of pyrolysis reactor used.
15. reactor_ignition_fuel
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Fuels used for reactor ignition.
16. pyrolysis_by_product
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Byproducts generated during pyrolysis.
17. h_c_ratio
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Hydrogen-to-carbon molar ratio of the biochar.
18. carbon_content_ratio
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Percentage of carbon content in biochar.
19. min_biochar_moisture_content
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Minimum moisture content in biochar.
20. avg_biochar_moisture_content
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Average moisture content in biochar.
21. calibrated_weighting_system
    1. **Data Type**: Boolean
    2. **Definition**: Indicates whether a well calibrated weighting system was used.
22. biochar_usage
    1. **Data Type**: List (Text)
    2. **Definition**: End-use applications for biochar.
23. biochar_donation
    1. **Data Type**: Boolean
    2. **Definition**: TRUE if the biochar is donated instead of sold.
24. feedstock_transport_distance_km
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Distance for transporting feedstock, in km.
25. usage_transport_distance_km
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Distance for transporting biochar for usage, in km.
26. lca_report_cadence
    1. **Data Type**: Text
    2. **Definition**: Frequency of the LCA computation on site.
27. gross_e_stored_kg_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: The total amount of carbon stored in the biochar per ton of dry biochar (in kg), calculated based on the biochar’s carbon content and molar ratios.
28. e_biomass_kg_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Emissions from biomass per ton of dry biochar (in kg).
29. e_production_kg_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Production emissions per ton of dry biochar (in kg).
30. e_use_kg_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Emissions from biochar usage (in kg).
31. feedstock_emission_kg_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Emissions from feedstock processing per ton of dry biochar (in kg).
32. electricity_emission_kg_per_ton_biochar
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Emissions from electricity usage per ton of biochar (in kg).
33. **net_e_stored_kg_per_ton_biochar**
    1. **Data Type**: Numerical
    2. **Definition**: Net carbon storage per ton of dry biochar (in kg). Calculated as: gross_e_stored_kg_per_ton_biochar - e_biomass_kg_per_ton_biochar - e_production_kg_per_ton_biochar - e_use_kg_per_ton_biochar

**Methodology**

1. **Document Analysis**:
    1. Carefully read the audit document to locate relevant data for each field.
    2. Look for exact phrases, numbers, or tables corresponding to the field definitions.
    3. For text field, try your best to give the most complete details.
2. **Data Verification**:
    1. Ensure each value is explicitly found in the document.
    2. For fields that are about weight, make sure that the unit of the value is correct (either in tons or kg).
    3. If a field requires calculation (e.g., eligible_credit_conversion_factor), compute it using data explicitly available in the document.
3. **Handling Missing Data**:
    1. If the information for field cannot be found or easily inferred from the document, leave it blank, don’t invent stuff.
4. **Output Format**:
    1. Provide the extracted data in a JSON object adhering strictly to the schema above.
    2. Ensure all keys are present, even if some values are blank.
